SAFETY COMES FIRST

⚠️ SAFETY GUIDELINES

Adventure is more enjoyable when you stay safe.

🥾 Before the Trek

✔ Check weather and trail conditions.

✔ Choose a suitable difficulty level.

✔ Inform someone about your route.

✔ Follow the organizer's instructions.

⛰️ During the Adventure

✔ Stay with your group.

✔ Drink water and take regular breaks.

✔ Follow marked trails and guide instructions.

✔ Avoid dangerous cliffs and wildlife.

🌿 Protect Nature

✔ Carry your waste back with you.

✔ Do not damage plants or disturb wildlife.

✔ Use designated campsites.

✔ Follow local fire safety rules.
